# KYC and Account Opening Policy
access: branch, risk

## Documents for individual accounts
Branch staff must collect PAN (or Form 60 where the customer has no PAN) and one officially valid document for proof of identity and address. Accepted documents are Aadhaar (with consent, masked to the last four digits), passport, driving licence, voter ID card and NREGA job card. A current photograph is captured at the branch.

## Video KYC
Video-based customer identification (V-CIP) may be used for resident individuals only. It is not available for non-resident (NRI) customers, minors or accounts opened under power of attorney. The session must be recorded, run by a trained officer from a bank location, and include live geo-tagging of the customer inside India.

## Periodic KYC update
KYC must be refreshed at least once every 2 years for high-risk customers, every 8 years for medium-risk customers and every 10 years for low-risk customers. If there is no change in KYC information, a self-declaration through the mobile app, internet banking, email or branch is sufficient.
