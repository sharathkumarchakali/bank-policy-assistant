# Card Dispute and Unauthorised Transaction Policy
access: branch, risk

## Customer liability for unauthorised transactions
If the customer reports an unauthorised electronic transaction within 3 working days of receiving the alert, customer liability is zero. If reported within 4 to 7 working days, liability is capped at INR 10,000 for savings accounts and INR 25,000 for credit cards. Reports after 7 working days are decided by the Card Disputes Committee.

## Shadow credit and resolution
The disputed amount must be credited to the customer's account as a shadow credit within 10 working days of the report. The dispute must be resolved, and the customer told the outcome, within 90 days of the report.

## How customers report
Customers can report through the 24x7 helpline, the mobile app "Block card" option, SMS reply to the alert, or at any branch. Staff must give the customer a complaint reference number with date and time of the report.
