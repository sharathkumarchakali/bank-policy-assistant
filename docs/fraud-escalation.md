# Fraud Risk Escalation Procedure (Restricted)
access: risk

## Escalation thresholds
Any suspected fraud involving INR 5 lakh or more must be escalated to the Fraud Risk Management Unit (FRMU) within 2 hours of detection. Suspected fraud below INR 5 lakh is logged in the fraud case tool and reviewed by the regional risk officer within 1 working day.

## Account freeze for suspected mule accounts
An account is placed on debit freeze when it receives credits from three or more unrelated customers who have reported fraud within 7 days, or when law enforcement sends a freeze request through the national cybercrime portal. Only the FRMU can lift a debit freeze.
