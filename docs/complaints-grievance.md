# Customer Complaints and Grievance Redressal Policy
access: branch, risk

## Acknowledgement and first response
Every complaint, in any channel, gets a reference number and an acknowledgement within 1 working day. Branches aim to resolve simple service complaints within 7 working days.

## Escalation
If the customer is not satisfied or the complaint is not resolved within 30 days, it is escalated to the Principal Nodal Officer. Staff must tell the customer they may approach the RBI Ombudsman if the complaint is rejected or not resolved within 30 days of lodging.
