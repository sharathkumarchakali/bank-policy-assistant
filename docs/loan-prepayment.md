# Retail Loan Prepayment and Foreclosure Policy
access: branch, risk

## Floating-rate loans
There is nil prepayment or foreclosure charge on floating-rate home loans and floating-rate personal loans taken by individuals, whatever the source of funds.

## Fixed-rate loans
For fixed-rate personal loans, a foreclosure charge of 3% of the outstanding principal applies if the loan is closed within the first 12 months, and 2% after that. Part-prepayment up to 25% of the outstanding principal once a year is free of charge.

## Closure documents
On full repayment, original property documents must be returned and the charge released within 30 days. The branch issues a no-dues certificate on the day of closure.
